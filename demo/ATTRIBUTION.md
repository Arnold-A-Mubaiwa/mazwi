# Demo recordings

The recordings in `recordings/` and their words in `demo.json` come from:

**Crowdsourced high-quality TTS data for four South African languages (OpenSLR 32)**
Copyright 2017 Google, Inc.; collected by North West University and Google.
https://www.openslr.org/32/
Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

> Daniel van Niekerk, Charl van Heerden, Marelie Davel, Neil Kleynhans, Oddur Kjartansson, Martin Jansche and Linne Ha.
> "Rapid development of TTS corpora for four South African languages." Interspeech 2017.

Changes: three clips (`xho_0050_0069105840`, `sso_1801_2816619422`, `tsn_4850_0027596674`) were converted from 48 kHz to 16 kHz mono. These converted files are shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

The speakers are volunteers from the original dataset, not Mazwi artists. The demo shows each sentence as a short story with its original words. No translations are included, because no speaker has checked one.
